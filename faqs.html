Getting started

What is MEDSETU?

MEDSETU brings two things together: real-time ECG monitoring, and one place for your medical records. It was built as a project at COEP Technological University to connect patients and doctors.

Who can use MEDSETU?

Patients who want to follow their heart health and keep their documents together, and doctors and healthcare professionals who need to review them.

Is MEDSETU free to use?

Yes. MEDSETU is a student prototype and everything in it is free to explore. There are no payments or subscriptions.

How do I log in?

Click Login, choose Patient or Doctor, then enter a username and password. In this prototype any details open the demo portal, and nothing you type is stored.

Health monitoring

What does MEDSETU monitor?

Your ECG: the electrical signal of your heart, read through skin electrodes by the MEDSETU ECG prototype. The live dashboard shows the filtered trace, heart rate, R-peaks and the signal range.

How does live data reach the dashboard?

The ECG device is Arduino-based. A Python script streams its filtered readings to the cloud, and the dashboard checks for new data every two seconds.

The dashboard says “No signal”. Is it broken?

No. It means the ECG device isn’t streaming at the moment. You can still open the patient database on the dashboard and review stored recordings.

Can I get a summary of my ECG?

Yes. On the dashboard, Generate report sends the recent signal to Google’s Gemini AI and returns a written summary you can download as a PDF.

It is AI-generated and for information only. Always talk to a licensed doctor.

Can I sync data from wearable devices?

Not yet. Monitoring currently works with the MEDSETU ECG prototype. Wearable integrations may come in a future version.

Is MEDSETU a medical device?

No. It is a working student prototype and is not certified for diagnosis. Use it to understand and share your data, not to replace a doctor.

Documents

What types of documents can I upload?

Medical reports, prescriptions, lab test results and other health documents, as PDF, JPEG or PNG files.

Can I share my documents with my doctor?

That’s what the doctor portal is for: it opens Access Documents, so a doctor can review the records their patient shares.

I uploaded the wrong document. How do I remove it?

Go to Upload Documents, find the file and use its delete option.

Why can’t I upload my document?

Check that the file is a PDF, JPEG or PNG, and that it is within the maximum file size. If it still fails, contact us.

Account & privacy

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

In this prototype, accounts aren’t stored, so there is nothing to reset: any username and password opens the demo portal.

How secure are my documents?

Because MEDSETU is a prototype, please don’t upload real medical documents. A production version would store records with encryption and access controls.
