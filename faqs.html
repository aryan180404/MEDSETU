§ 1The project

What is MEDSETU?

An ECG monitoring project: an Arduino-based ECG reads the heart’s signal, a Python script filters and streams it, and a web dashboard draws it live, with an AI-written reading on request.

Who built it, and why?

Aryan Agarwal, as an Instrumentation & Control project at COEP Technological University, to make real-time heart data easy to see and share.

Is MEDSETU a medical device?

No. It is a working student prototype and isn’t certified for diagnosis. Use it to understand an ECG, not to replace a doctor.

What happened to the patient and doctor logins?

They were part of an early concept and have been retired so the project shows what was actually built. A records vault for documents is listed under what’s next.

§ 2How it works

How is the heart signal measured?

Electrodes on the right and left arm pick up the voltage between them, known as Lead I. The Arduino board amplifies that signal and samples it with its analog-to-digital converter.

How do readings reach the browser?

A Python script cleans each reading and sends it to a Google Sheet. The dashboard fetches the newest rows every two seconds and redraws the trace.

Why was noise such a big deal?

At the skin, a heartbeat is around a thousandth of a volt, so mains hum and other interference can swamp it. The prototype uses careful grounding and EMF/EMI mitigation to keep the ADC readings clean.

How is heart rate worked out?

The dashboard finds the tall R peaks in the recent window of samples and converts their count into beats per minute. When the stream sends its own analysis, the dashboard shows that instead.

§ 3The live dashboard

Why does it say “No active stream”?

The ECG device isn’t sending data at the moment. That’s normal: it only streams while it’s switched on and wired up. The patient database still works.

Who are the patients in the database?

Nobody real. The 50 records are synthetic demo data generated in the browser, each with its own ECG recording, so you can explore the dashboard any time.

What does “Generate report” do?

It sends the recent signal (or a patient’s recording) to Google’s Gemini AI and shows a written summary: the pattern, the heart rate, anything unusual, and suggestions.

It’s AI-generated and informational only. Always talk to a licensed doctor.

Can I keep the report?

Yes. Download PDF saves the report, with its time and report ID, as a one-page A4 file.

§ 4Data & contact

Is any personal data stored?

There are no accounts. The live stream stores ECG readings with timestamps, and the demo patients are generated on the fly. Please don’t send real medical records.
