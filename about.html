A bridge between a heartbeat and the people reading it.

Setu means bridge. MEDSETU began at COEP Technological University with a simple question: why can’t patients see their own heart data in real time, and why are their records scattered everywhere?

Where it came from.

MEDSETU was built on a foundation in Instrumentation and Control Engineering, IoT and data science. It brings ECG monitoring, cloud-based data and clear visualisation into one working system.

What began as an academic project points at remote healthcare, telemedicine and long-term monitoring: a step toward care that is more connected and more patient-centred.

  1. 1

    Instrumentation

    An Arduino ECG with careful grounding and interference shielding.

  2. 2

    Software

    A Python filter and streamer, and a browser dashboard that reads it live.

  3. 3

    Validation

    Usability and system checks with 8–10 people.
